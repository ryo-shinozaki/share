## 英語

## AI, Data Analytics, and Mathematical Optimization

### Advancing Manufacturing Technologies and Developing Internal Systems

We conduct research and development in manufacturing technologies using AI and data analytics, and improve manufacturing processes through mathematical optimization. We are also integrating AI into our internal systems to make better use of the company's data and knowledge. By sharing what we learn through trial and error and problem-solving, both within and outside the company, we continually refine our technologies and expertise.
